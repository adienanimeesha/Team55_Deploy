## Value Proposition Canvas

---

### VPC 1 — Segment 1: Students New to UQ Campus

*(First-year undergraduates, transfer students, and newly arrived international students)*

**Customer Jobs**

- Get to classes on time during the first weeks of semester, when buildings, rooms, and routes are still unfamiliar.

- Figure out where classrooms are on a large campus — not just the building, but the exact room inside it.

- Keep track of class schedules and assignment deadlines across multiple courses using fragmented tools (Blackboard, my.UQ, personal calendars, email).

- Build a reliable daily routine for moving around campus so that navigation becomes automatic rather than a daily planning task.

- Feel confident and independent when navigating the university environment, rather than relying on friends or strangers for directions.

- Access course materials, announcements, and submissions efficiently through a single platform rather than checking multiple sources.

**Customer Pains**

- No single university platform integrates timetables, deadlines, and campus navigation, forcing students to switch between three or more tools for one task.

- Estimated walk times are inaccurate and do not account for finding the exact room, stairs, crowded areas, or confusing building layouts.

- UQ Maps is either completely unknown to students or perceived as difficult to use, so its localised campus value is not reaching them.

- Google Maps gives indirect or inaccurate routes on campus, does not recognise UQ building numbers, and does not route through campus shortcuts.

- Many students do not know the Blackboard mobile app exists, so they never receive push notifications from the university's primary academic platform.

- Blackboard notifications, when received, are poorly formatted and perceived as spammy rather than actionable.

- Anxiety and stress about being late to class, occurring both before departure and during the walk, especially for attendance-marked sessions.

- Having to rely on friends or strangers for directions, which creates dependency and undermines independence.

**Customer Gains**

- One integrated view showing what class, where it is, and when to leave — eliminating the need to cross-reference multiple platforms.

- Confidence to navigate campus independently from the first day, without relying on friends or leaving excessively early as a buffer.

- Reduced first-week stress and a smoother transition into university life, targeting the period when navigation difficulty peaks.

- Arriving on time without wasting time on excessive buffer margins that could be spent studying or resting.

- Less daily cognitive load spent on planning logistics like checking timetables, looking up locations, and estimating travel times.

**Products & Services**

- An integrated class schedule view within Blackboard that displays timetable entries alongside building locations and room numbers.

- Direct navigation links from each timetable entry to UQ Maps, providing campus-specific routing to the exact building and room.

- Pre-departure planning with recommended leave times calculated from the travel distance between consecutive classes.

- A timeline slider feature that lets students visualise where they should be at each point between classes, enabling pre-planned journeys.

- Context-sensitive push notifications for upcoming class departures, delivered via the Blackboard mobile app.

**Pain Relievers**

- Eliminates platform fragmentation by combining schedule, location, and navigation in one place within Blackboard.

- Surfaces UQ Maps automatically through timetable links, so students benefit from campus-specific routing without discovering a separate app.

- Provides travel estimates that account for campus-specific factors beyond raw walking distance, reducing the gap between estimated and actual arrival time.

- Delivers well-formatted, relevant departure reminders instead of generic notification spam, giving students a reason to download and keep the Blackboard app.

- Reduces pre-departure and mid-walk anxiety by providing clear guidance about when to leave and whether the plan is on track.

- Supports independent navigation so students do not need to depend on friends or strangers for directions.

**Gain Creators**

- Gives new students a single starting point for their daily campus routine from day one — no setup, no separate apps, no learning curve.

- Builds navigation confidence by providing campus-specific directions and room-level guidance that generic map apps cannot match.

- Reclaims time currently lost to excessive buffer margins by providing accurate, context-aware departure recommendations.

- Reduces daily cognitive load by automating the "where, when, how long" calculation that students currently perform manually.

- Creates a positive first impression of the university's digital support, signalling that UQ actively helps students settle in.
