## Interview 4
*Person4*

**Eric:** Are you a domestic or international student, what year are you in, and are you doing a single or double degree?  
**Person 4** I am a international student in my second year and I am doing single degree. I have been study here for a while so I am familiar with the campus.

**Eric:** Walk me through how you keep track of your classes and deadlines week to week — what do you use and why?  
**Person 4** I mainly use my mail calendar to keep tracking of everythings. At the start of the semaster, I add all my assessments and important dates. During the week, I check it regularly so reminders really help me stay organized and not forget deadlines.

**Eric:** How do you access your course materials, announcements and assignment submissions — what platform or app do you use for that?  
**Person 4** I usually use Blackboard to access materials and check announcements and submit assignments. I check it often, especially when I know there are deadlines on new contents coming up.

**Eric:** At the start of a new semester, how do you figure out where all your new classes are — what does that first week look like?  
**Person 4** I usually check the timetable first, then look up to the building location online. In the first week I try to go to campus a bit earlyer so I have enough time to find the class room. It can be a bit of coufusing at the begining.

**Eric:** When you have a class in a building you haven't been to before, walk me through what you do — from when you first check where it is to when you actually get there.  
**Person 4** If it is a new building, I usually search it on Google Maps and UQ Maps and check how long would it takes to walk there. When I know a way, I check the Maps again to make sure I am going on the right direciton.

**Eric:** Have you ever used UQ Maps — if yes, what for? If not, what have you used instead?  
**Person 4** Yes I used UQ Maps finding buildings on campus, it is helpful. But I use Google Maps more often, because I am more familiar with it.

**Eric:** Tell me about a time you were worried you wouldn't make it to class on time — walk me through what happened, and what actually came of it.  
**Person 4** There was one time I had back to back classes but I doesn't sure how far apart they were. I felt a bit stress. while I was leaving, I keep checking the Maps and walk faster. In the end, I made it on time but too stressful.

**Eric:** When you check how long a walk will take, does that feel like enough information — or is there something it doesn't tell you?  
**Person 4** I think it is helpful but not always enough. It doesn't show things like stairs or crowded area which can affect how fast I can walk. Also sometimes, the estimated time isn't very accurate.
