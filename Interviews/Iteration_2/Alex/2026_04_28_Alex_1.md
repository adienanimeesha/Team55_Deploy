## Interview 1  
*Person 1*

**Alex:** Are you a domestic or international student?  
**Person 1:** Domestic.  

**Alex:** And what year are you in?  
**Person 1:** Third year.  

**Alex:** Okay. Can you walk me through what you do when you are between classes and unsure if you will make it on time?  
**Person 1:** A lot of the time between classes I'll go home because I ride to uni. But if not, then I'll sit around and read my book.  

**Alex:** Has Google Maps or Apple Maps or any navigation app you use ever given you unclear information on campus?  
**Person 1:** Yeah, definitely.  

**Alex:** Maybe what happened?  
**Person 1:** I know I also used UQ Maps for a little bit, but that one also didn't really work very well. It gives you the right building, but inside the building it doesn't really map it very well.  

**Alex:** We are building something that tells you whether you will make it between classes before you start walking. Does that sound like something you would use?  
**Person 1:** Yeah, definitely.  

**Alex:** What would make you open this instead of Google Maps or others or UQ Maps?  
**Person 1:** If it could be more accurate for where exactly my classroom was. So accuracy is probably the most important part.  

**Alex:** Even in weeks where you already know your routes well, do you think something like this would still be useful?  
**Person 1:** Yeah, I think so.  

**Alex:** And are there situations where you find it more useful than others?  
**Person 1:** I would say at the start of the semester when I don't know where my classes are, it's probably the most useful. And also when it's exploring different parts of the Uni. So like in the engineering area, I don't know it as well.  

**Alex:** So here is our draft. So you can see it. And you can scroll.  
**Person 1:** That looks awesome.  

**Alex:** Of everything you just saw, what is the one thing you could actually use and what would you cut?  
**Person 1:** I would say probably the coolest one is like telling you where to go inside the building. So like go up the stairs to the right as like an example. In terms of what to cut, I mean I don't think I would use the notifications as much, me personally. I would probably just check it and then go there and then once I get to the building I would check it again.  

**Alex:** Maybe this will disturb your normally use phone.  
**Person 1:** Yeah.  

**Alex:** Okay, thank you very much.  
**Person 1:** No problem.  

**Alex:** Have a good day.  
**Person 1:** You too.
