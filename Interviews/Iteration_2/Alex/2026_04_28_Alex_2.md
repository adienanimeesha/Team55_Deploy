## Interview 2  
*Person 2*

**Alex:** Do you want to sit down?  
**Person 2:** Okay, thank you.  

**Alex:** So, are you a domestic or international student?  
**Person 2:** Domestic.  

**Alex:** What year are you in?  
**Person 2:** Third year.  

**Alex:** Okay. Can you walk me through what you do when you are between classes and unsure if you will make it on time?  
**Person 2:** I usually just don't go. If I think I'm running out of time.  

**Alex:** Okay. Has Google Maps or other map app you used ever given you unclear information on campus?  
**Person 2:** Yes. I think in my first year I got lost a little bit. But now I know my way around.  

**Alex:** We are building something that tells you whether you will make it between classes before you start walking. Does that sound like something you will use?  
**Person 2:** Yeah.  

**Alex:** What would make you open this app instead of Google Maps or other maps?  
**Person 2:** If it shows that it would make it there on time, I would probably use that more than the other ones. Because I don't really indicate that.  

**Alex:** Oh. So, even in weeks where you already know your routes well, do you think something like this would still be useful?  
**Person 2:** If I already know my class well? Yeah. Maybe I would use it a little bit less. But it could be if I was at home and deciding whether to go into uni or just miss the class.  

**Alex:** Okay. So, here is some of our draft. You can scroll it and watch it. And it shows you how.  
**Person 2:** Oh, yeah. Yeah.  

**Alex:** And what would you use as a card?  
**Person 2:** Um... Oh, you can put in. I probably wouldn't use the directions. Because I already know how to get there.  

**Alex:** Yeah.  
**Person 2:** But I would use it if it shows me how long it takes to get there.  

**Alex:** The time?  
**Person 2:** Yes. Because sometimes if I'm at home or on the other side of uni, and I don't think I'll make it to the class in time, then that would be a way to check instead of just missing the class.  

**Alex:** Okay. That's all. Thank you very much.  
**Person 2:** No worries. Have a good day.  

**Alex:** Have a good day. Yes, you too.
